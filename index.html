How to play

  1. The highlighted team chooses a tile. Press Start for a 20-second clue.
  2. Select the answering team. Press Correct to add points instantly and show the answer.
  3. If wrong, press Incorrect → Steal, then select another team. They get a fresh 20 seconds. Wrong answers lose no points.
  4. Add Bonus +5 for an explanation, if earned. Press Next to mark played and advance.
  5. Highest score after 20 clues wins. Use Undo for corrections.

Accept alternatives with a clear biblical connection. “Show answer” ends stealing. Progress saves in this browser.
